Child Safety Standards

SOMEONE App — ONA PROJECT
Effective date: November 5, 2026

Our Commitment

SOMEONE (the "App") is committed to the safety and protection of all minors. We have a zero-tolerance policy toward any content or behavior that sexually exploits or endangers children.

Prohibited Content and Conduct

The following are strictly prohibited on SOMEONE:

  • Child Sexual Abuse Material (CSAM): Any content that sexually exploits minors is absolutely forbidden and will be reported to the appropriate authorities.
  • Child Sexual Abuse and Exploitation (CSAE): Any behavior, content, or communication that constitutes or facilitates the sexual abuse or exploitation of a child is prohibited.
  • Grooming: Any attempt to build a relationship with a minor for the purpose of sexual exploitation is prohibited.
  • Inappropriate content involving minors: Any sexual or sexually suggestive content involving persons under the age of 18 is prohibited.

Age Requirement

SOMEONE requires all users to be at least 17 years of age. Age verification is required during account registration. Users found to be under the minimum age will have their accounts removed immediately.

AI Character Policy

All AI characters on SOMEONE are adults (18 years of age or older). No AI character may be represented, described, or portrayed as a minor. Users may not request the App to generate any content depicting minors in a sexual or inappropriate manner.

Reporting

If you encounter any content or behavior that you believe violates this Child Safety Standard, please report it immediately to:

Email: someoneapp.support@gmail.com

All reports are treated seriously and reviewed promptly. Accounts found in violation will be terminated and, where required by law, reported to the National Center for Missing & Exploited Children (NCMEC) or relevant authorities.

Enforcement

Violations of this policy will result in:

  • Immediate removal of the offending content
  • Permanent suspension of the offending account
  • Reporting to law enforcement and child safety organizations where legally required

Contact

For questions about this policy, contact us at someoneapp.support@gmail.com.
