Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 18, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how SOMEONE ("the App", "we", "us", or "our") collects, uses, and shares information about you when you use our mobile application.

1. Information We Collect

We collect the following personal information when you register and use the App:

  • Email address — used for account creation and login
  • Display name — the name you choose to go by in the App
  • Date of birth — used to verify you meet our minimum age requirement (17+)
  • Conversation data — messages you send and receive within the App, used to provide and improve the AI companion experience

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To provide and operate the App's features
  • To personalize your experience with AI companions
  • To process in-app purchases through Apple App Store and Google Play
  • To send push notifications (you can opt out in device settings)
  • To improve the App's performance and safety

3. In-App Purchases

The App offers optional in-app purchases (subscriptions and credit packs) processed by Apple App Store and Google Play. We do not store your payment card information. All payment transactions are handled by Apple or Google under their respective privacy policies.

4. Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. We may share data with:

  • Anthropic — AI responses are powered by Anthropic's Claude model. Your messages are processed according to Anthropic's Privacy Policy.
  • ElevenLabs — voice synthesis for text-to-speech features
  • Cloudflare R2 — image storage
  • fal.ai — AI image generation
  • Service providers who assist in operating the App, under confidentiality obligations

5. Data Retention

We retain your account data for as long as your account is active. You may request deletion of your account and associated data by contacting us. Conversation history may be retained in anonymized form for service improvement.

6. Age Requirement

The App is intended for users aged 17 and older. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone under 17. If we become aware that a user is under 17, we will delete their account.

7. Your Rights (GDPR)

If you are located in the European Economic Area, you have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you
  • Request correction of inaccurate data
  • Request deletion of your data
  • Object to or restrict processing of your data
  • Data portability

To exercise these rights, please contact us at the email below.

8. Security

We use industry-standard security measures including encrypted connections (HTTPS) and secure database storage to protect your information.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of significant changes through the App or by email. Continued use of the App after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
